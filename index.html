Why you’re seeing this

30,000+ apps taught us what moves conversion. Here’s what we’d test first at Pocket FM.

Adapty runs in-app purchases and subscriptions for more than 30,000 apps, so we see which onboarding and paywall changes lift revenue across the whole market, not just inside one app.

We pointed that at Pocket FM. We took your own screens, copy and prices from screenshots of your live iOS store, your App Store listing and public reviews, and rebuilt the path from install to the first coin purchase around the five ideas with the most potential.
