# Pocket FM Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/pocketfm-flow-rebuild-v2/**

Five ideas to turn more Pocket FM users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Pocket FM's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Open on the show from their trailer | +10–20% CR to first purchase |
| 02 | Lead the first unlock with your $0.99 Trial Pack | +10–15% CR to first purchase |
| 03 | Ask at the cliffhanger, inside the player | +5–10% CR to first purchase |
| 04 | Price packs in episodes, and recommend one | +5–10% ARPPU |
| 05 | Catch the people who close it with your ad packs | +3–8% CR to first purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Pocket FM's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ apps, not measured lift for Pocket FM. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
